On Duty
Framework

Standalone LSPDFR-style police duty for FiveM — CAD, traffic stops, pursuits, MDT, and an in-game Callout Builder. No ESX or QBCore.

Get started Browse features
Resource onduty Version 0.29.0 Lua 5.4

What you get

Duty & MDT

Callsigns, specialties, freemode or duty ped, movable HUD, and a full tablet MDT.

CAD Dispatch

Weighted callouts, multi-unit attach, stand-downs, panic board-clear, and radio audio.

Stops & Chases

AI pull-overs, foot and vehicle pursuits, search mode, PIT, tackle, and surrender.

Callout Builder

Author multi-role scenes in MDT — actors, questions, vehicles, briefing, test spawn.

Quick start

ensure onduty

Then set Config.Admin.passcode in shared/config.lua, restart, and go on duty with a 3-digit callsign (F4).
